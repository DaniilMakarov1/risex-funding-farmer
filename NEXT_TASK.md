- Immutable entry quote; request cancel at five seconds after activation or first partial. Primary activation/cancel/taker delays 500 ms; stress 1000 ms plus 1 bp on every RISEx fill. Processing-ready governs reaction; delayed processing cannot erase fills already received in the active interval. Capture actual decision-ready in whichever offline driver produces decisions; never substitute earlier book receipts or use future books. No ambiguous possible fill becomes clean NO_FILL/FLAT.
- After entry effective cancellation, hedge accumulated quantity after taker delay (a fully filled entry has no remaining maker quantity to cancel). Fill only grid/minimum/depth-executable quantity. Unmatched RISEx residue takes a delayed taker unwind if executable, otherwise remains explicitly unresolved. No silent rounding, unlimited retry or fictitious closing liquidity.

Apply the same hypothetical activation and post-only eligibility contract. Invalid eligibility routes to forced unwind; no expected exit profit is booked in advance.
- First exit partial requests cancel; its corresponding Lighter quantity closes after delay; remaining paired quantity unwinds after effective cancellation. Max hold is 120 seconds from the first maker fill: cancel outstanding maker quote, then force out remaining positions. Account for every late partial and pending action without duplicate closes or new entries during closing. No position-increasing over-close.
- Ledger records each hypothetical fill, venue/side, exact quantity/notional, maker/taker fee, reason and evidence/timing identity. Completed execution-only PnL is signed cash-flow sum less all fees including unmatched/forced unwinds; an incomplete cash flow is not terminal PnL. Track turnover, holding/occupancy and unmatched exposure duration, normal/forced/aborted/unresolved states for S3 reporting. Primary/stress remain alternatives, never summed.
- Funding stays UNKNOWN; no funding accrual engine or full-profitability claim. Gap, stale/missing required book, uncertain order, non-executable residue or terminal pending state yields explicit insufficient/unresolved evidence. Bounded halt is sufficient; no speculative recovery.
