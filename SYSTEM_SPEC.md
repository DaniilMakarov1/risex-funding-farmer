The HCR-44 candidate is a separate, one-session read-only Robinhood stream collector. It uses the configured BTC market 1 and two account identities, Robinhood's documented `wss://api.rh.lighter.xyz/stream?readonly=true`, `order_book/1` and two authenticated `account_all_orders/{account}` subscriptions. Authentication uses the existing protected Keychain boundary and pinned SDK's no-op nonce manager; it sends only subscribe messages and transport keepalives, never a transaction. The current owner-authorized session ceiling is 8 MiB per decompressed frame, 500 frames, 16 MiB total processed input and 30 seconds, with a one-message receive queue. It persists only allowlisted book continuity, private subscription/snapshot counters, and exact order identity/status/filled/remaining projections with local monotonic receipt time and optional raw venue timestamps. A private snapshot counter requires an exact subscribed frame with a structurally valid orders map and remains narrower than event/history completeness. Private raw frames, auth tokens and signatures are never persisted. An unanchored book update is not a snapshot; nonce mismatch marks a gap; reconnect invalidates continuity without resetting budgets. Private event completeness remains unproved, and stream events never authorize trading admission or cancellation. The previous 64 KiB ceiling caused a locally sent close code 1009; the new larger bounded measurement is separate from production and does not itself validate trading use.

HCR-43 candidate keeps the existing HTTP send and exact REST admission path for both paired opening and reduce-only paired closing. The production HTTP mutation wrapper now records only numeric local milestones: session readiness, aiohttp connection queue/setup or reuse when signaled, request headers/body callback signals, response headers, first body byte when available, complete body and JSON parse. These callbacks are local transport observations, not proof of wire delivery or matching-engine acceptance; missing signals remain absent. Prepared order diagnostics enter the existing latency record, and cancellation nonce/signing/transport diagnostics enter its result journal. No token, signed body or private response frame is included in these timings. After receiver terminal observation, the exact source lookup immediately preceding cancellation is reused for the cancellation freshness/identity guard, eliminating the formerly duplicated exact REST lookup in that path. All other cancellation paths retain their own exact recheck, and actual cancellation completion still requires reconciliation. This candidate has no WS-driven trading admission, no new sender, and no live latency or matching claim.

The HCR-43 correction candidate also includes an offline-only bounded frame parser with no network, credential or mutation interface. It requires exact integer types for configured and inbound market/account/client identities and connection epochs; booleans, floats and strings never become exact identities through Python equality. A local monotonic receipt time cannot regress from the last accepted frame, including across reconnect; equal times are allowed. A regression stops observation without recording the later terminal event, and reconnect never resets the original duration, byte or frame budgets. The parser requires a public-book subscription snapshot and book-local nonce continuity, marks gaps and private status conflicts, and never claims private stream completeness or trading authority. This is local observation logic, not venue event-order proof or a connected collector.

## Product and boundaries

The active utility runs an explicitly requested finite two-account Robinhood Chain perpetual cycle through the public order book. One account places LIMIT/POST_ONLY; the other sends opposite MARKET/IOC with an explicit price bound. There is no named-counterparty reservation, native position transfer or cross-account atomicity. Other participants can fill either order between observation and dispatch; faster preparation reduces exposure time but cannot guarantee exclusion.

Use Robinhood's own API/identity/signing domain (api.rh.lighter.xyz, domain 466324); ordinary Lighter is not an implicit substitute. The adapter pins lighter-sdk 1.1.2. The current operator configuration is BTC and two fixed accounts; lower-level configurable interfaces do not authorize new markets/runs. Only an owner-triggered new cycle may configure selected-market cross leverage as described below. No transfer or adoption of historical inventory is implicit.

## Normal cycle

1. `./start` reads local configuration and asks for one Enter confirmation; cancel/help is offline and credential-free. A fresh private-owner Telegram `/run` supplies equivalent confirmation. Validate local configuration/evidence, storage and SDK before claiming a slot.
2. Draw uniformly among the two configured first accounts and BUY/SELL: four equally likely combinations. Source is the first LIMIT account; receiver is the other. Internal LONG means receiver BUY/source SELL, SHORT means receiver SELL/source BUY. Persist immutable route and unique client prefix in a new exclusive owner-only `cycle-NNN` slot; fsync file and directory before further access. Existing slots are never reused. Explicit-plan interfaces keep their supplied route.
3. Require exact initial zero positions and no conflicting selected-market orders, correct account/market/environment identity, readiness and fresh available balances. Sample quantity once uniformly in legal integer size ticks, bounded below by venue minimums and above by four times the smaller fresh available balance divided by opening price. Sample/persist one integer hold of 20–180 seconds. Never redraw route, quantity or hold on retries.
4. For the selected quantity, budget each account's fresh mark-based initial-margin notional, the higher of published all-tier Robinhood and observed fee rates at its worst executable price, and adverse entry-to-mark PnL. Compute the largest supported integer initial-margin fraction that fits its own fresh available balance (the lowest modeled leverage), limited to 1x–4x and the fresh market minimum fraction. Read the current selected-market cross setting; when different, journal one setting intent, send one signed update, then require fresh exact readback before any order. A rejection stops before opening; an ambiguous send or unproved readback blocks future `/run` without replay until proved terminal. If the second account fails, leave the first confirmed setting visible and send no order; no automatic rollback. Current reserved cross margin is not a new opening requirement and is never compared with free available balance. An independently evidenced incremental opening requirement still blocks when it exceeds free balance. The budget does not prove the venue's complete risk/admission rule; the venue may reject the order. No quantity redraw, setting retry or leverage increase during closure follows a rejection.
   The HCR-42 budget records both accounts before either leg's margin refusal, with exact model components, observed/target setting and provenance. Original numeric budget values retain their original account/metadata/book observation identities and times through leverage-setting readback and pair retries; setting/preparation reference times are distinct. Missing or conflicting provenance cannot produce an unproved initial-to-fresh delta. A confirmed shortage is visible in terminal and saved Telegram reports; an unknown component remains unknown. The owner-selected opening policy is `OpeningMarginReserve(initial_quote=Decimal('0.10'), dispatch_quote=Decimal('0.02'))`, explicitly bound by the operator file for both terminal and Telegram opening paths. Both amounts are quote/balance currency **per account**, additional to fee and adverse entry-to-mark loss: retain at least 0.10 after initial sizing and integer IMF choice and at least 0.02 after the final fresh costs, allowing equality. The 0.08 difference may absorb intervening changes. The unchanged absolute owner notional cap, venue minimum, one quantity draw, 1x–4x setting and exact readback remain. No automatic reserve/setting increase, quantity redraw or price widening follows a refusal. The choice is not a proved optimum and does not guarantee venue admission or matching. Opening reserve never blocks explicit reduce-only residual closure. This policy requires a reviewed operator-file update and runtime activation separately from Git publication.
5. Select an exclusive one-tick source improvement strictly inside the valid two-sided spread; receiver bound equals source price. A one-tick spread that merely joins existing volume is nonqualifying. Recheck exact grids, minimum quantity/notional, available balance, confirmed fraction and source/receiver bound compatibility; never round exposure upward.
6. Place source first; admit receiver only through the exact checks below. HOLD begins only after full reciprocal matching of the selected quantity between the two own orders is proved. Expected positions reached through outside trades do not authorize HOLD.
7. Wait using monotonic elapsed time from persisted confirmation anchor. At the deadline attempt paired closure with reversed sides, both reduce-only, fresh mirrored source price and equal receiver bound. The hold deadline is planned closing start, not a promise of full closure.
8. Reconcile every mutation. Fully known incomplete opening skips HOLD and uses existing residual closure. Preserve strategy failure even if residual recovery ultimately proves both accounts flat. Stop after this cycle; no automatic next cycle or replay after restart.

## Preparation, admission and timing

Before each final quote, overlap independent metadata/account reads and reserve one unsent nonce per configured account/key. SDK account state and active orders overlap; keep the account response's original timestamp. Fetch final book after validated context, then recheck its ages. The child consumes this exact preflight context once without duplicate post-quote account requests. Sign both exact final plans before exposing source when preparation is supported.

Prepared mutations stay in memory, owned by the original adapter/account/key and immutable plan; reserve/consume once. Unsent reservations expire within freshness bounds and release on failure/cancellation. Drain sibling tasks before cleanup. Possibly transmitted nonces remain blocked. Serialize signing/nonce/cancellation within each account/key domain. No prepared payload, token or secret enters durable evidence.

Receiver admission needs fresh independently read accounts and a two-sided public book, then a final exact source lookup. Source must be active, zero-fill and match account, market, order/client IDs, side, LIMIT/POST_ONLY, reduce-only flag, price and full remaining quantity. Its public level must identify that exact order/owner/side/price/quantity. Better external volume is LOST; same-price volume without explicit FIFO proof, anonymous/missing/stale/future/malformed/conflicting evidence is UNKNOWN. Both block receiver. An already-filled source always blocks receiver; no opening chase.

The HCR-42 timing view reuses local phase durations and separately reports each proof's age at guard/receiver send. Visibility-window duration overlaps private order, account and public-book reads; it is not the publication instant of a public level. A final private lookup does not renew public price-priority proof. No stream replacement or faster live outcome is established by the candidate.

One bounded refresh may resolve specifically pre-visibility missing source evidence only when earlier observations prove correct identities, unchanged positions, readiness, margin, freshness and no conflicting orders/fills. If the source account already identifies the exact resting source and only the public level is absent, refresh only the book; retain both account snapshots and their original ages. Otherwise refresh accounts and book together. Contradictory evidence remains sticky. Final admission revalidates all observations and reads the exact source again. Final public-book age participates in a monotonic send deadline with account, metadata, exact-order and prepared evidence, including nonce/signing/transmission time. Expiry does not authorize replay.

Default bounds: freshness 10 s; request 5 s; order wait 10 s; reconciliation 20 s; polling 0.5 s; maximum 40 polls; source exchange expiry 300 s; auth lifetime 600 s. Explicit valid overrides remain supported. Optional max_quote_age_seconds and max_source_to_receiver_seconds are absent by default and, if configured, positive and no greater than freshness. Offline timing does not calibrate live thresholds.

Preparation and safe zero-fill paired retries share at most three attempts per opening or closing. Retry transient read/preparation failures only before possible mutation; deterministic configuration/identity/auth/state/grid/minimum failures stop. After source exposure, only complete terminal zero-fill/empty-history reconciliation, unchanged original positions, no receiver dispatch, no ambiguous mutation and no other barrier permit a fresh pair attempt after exact source cancellation or canceled-post-only. Use fresh quote, new IDs and separate child journal. Arbitrary cancellations and partial fills do not qualify. Closing retries retain the nonzero paired baseline.

## Reconciliation and residual closure

Persist and fully synchronize mutation intent before a single send without redirects/implicit retries. Accepted response/tx hash does not prove execution. Exception/timeout after send entry, malformed/conflicting response or missing terminal is unresolved; no dependent write on either account. Exact known application rejection and local pre-send failure are distinct. Restart reads/reconciles; it never replays mutations or advances automatically.

Only cancel the exact positively identified own source. Count cancellation-race fills. Require bounded complete order/trade/account history, exact deduplication, terminal order and causal position agreement. Temporarily propagating observations may be reread within existing limits; identity/contract failures remain barriers. An absent source or a source filled during cancellation after a failed public-book guard can become known source-only PARTIAL only after full terminal order/trade/position reconciliation and an unchanged undispatched receiver. Preserve the admission reason, but do not classify proved execution as UNKNOWN merely because that reason contains UNKNOWN. Unrelated identity, history, cancellation or transport uncertainty still blocks dependent writes.

Retain a positively observed source order ID when a later exact lookup temporarily returns no order, including when the accepted response has no order ID. Reconcile history against that retained ID; a conflicting later ID remains a barrier. Missing visibility never permits receiver dispatch or proves cancellation.

After complete reconciliation, close freshly proved residuals with reduce-only bounded MARKET/IOC, fresh executable-side prices and unique IDs. Cancel/reconcile a remaining own limit first. Reconcile each attempt before sizing the next actual residual; never overlap unresolved orders. Pace terminal zero-fill attempts by the existing poll interval and serve both accounts fairly. Fully reconciled partial fills have no fixed total attempt cap. Exchange rejection, ineligible below-minimum residual, invalid/stale/ambiguous state, failed request or interruption stops dependent writes and preserves PARTIAL/UNKNOWN. No widening or size increase/reversal.

HCR-41 reserves a nonce before the residual-close book read when supported, rechecks the exact account, and binds signing and transport to the earliest evidence deadline. A known local nonce/signing failure blocks only that account after fresh two-account identity/position reconciliation; the independent account may still close. An uncertain send is never replayed. Transport and terminal/reconciliation phase timings are separate diagnostics, not venue latency or a live fill guarantee. Leverage intents record signed hash/nonce/account/key/type before send, preserving the pinned SDK's exact 80-character bare hexadecimal hash without adding a prefix or normalizing case. A proven pre-transport failure, including cancellation during nonce/signing/lock preparation, is journaled as NOT_SENT; cancellation still propagates. Cancellation after possible sendTx entry stays UNKNOWN. An uncertain setting can clear only after bounded read-only proof of exact terminal executed or failed transaction identity, consumed API-key nonce and fresh flat/no-active-order account state; executed status also requires the exact requested setting readback. The separate version-2 checkpoint binds original history and persists this sequencer soft-finality proof across restarts. Pending/conflicting/incomplete evidence blocks `/run`; `/close` remains available for proved inventory. REST book request start/end and optional venue timestamp/version are diagnostic; matching-engine continuity remains unproved.

The HCR-41 opening budget uses fresh mark notional times the selected initial-margin fraction, the higher of published all-tier Robinhood and observed fee rates times worst executable price, and adverse entry-to-mark PnL for both accounts. It caps the single quantity draw by both budgets and the owner's original absolute 4x cap, then recalculates with fresh account, mark, fee and quote evidence before the source LIMIT. Missing mark or a changed budget blocks before send. Published fees and this model do not prove the venue's complete admission rule; no discretionary reserve is enabled.

Reduce-only paired and residual closing requires fresh authorized account/position/order identity, but does not compare existing `cross_initial_margin_requirement` with remaining `available_balance` or require new opening margin. A close is still subject to the venue's own acceptance, price bound and full causal reconciliation.

For Robinhood BTC market 1 only, exact full-residual reduce-only MARKET/IOC closure may submit an on-grid quantity below catalog base/notional minimums. Actual venue history confirmed filled initial quantities 0.00004 and 0.00007 BTC below catalog 0.00020 BTC / 10 quote; this is observed behavior, not a general documented exemption or guaranteed acceptance. Journal the exception, quantity and current minimums. All opening/paired checks and other markets retain their minimum gates. Never round a residual up; rejection/unknown execution still stops that operation normally.

Recovery account reads may repeat only classified transport/time-out failures within the existing reconciliation timeout, poll interval and maximum poll count. Drain parallel reads, retain identity failures even alongside transport errors, and never repeat a mutation. Revalidate quote freshness after a delayed pre-send account read. Preserve the specific local refusal in terminal evidence, including failures before the first closing intent.

## Result and accounting contract

- Paired SUCCESS requires full exact quantity on both legs and reciprocal receipts agreeing on trade ID, accounts, order IDs, price, quantity and opposite sides; supplied client identities must agree. Missing/unproved/external quantity cannot become own matching. Full normal-cycle SUCCESS additionally requires successful opening and closing and terminal inventory proof. CLOSE_REOPEN retains its separate exposure semantics.
- CONFIRMED_FLAT requires resolved terminal order histories and fresh causal exact-zero positions on both accounts; snapshots or process exit alone are insufficient. Known residual is PARTIAL, unresolved execution UNKNOWN. Fees and matching do not replace inventory evidence.
- Receipts retain actual quantities/prices and fee evidence. At the official SDK boundary, derive maker/taker role from strict is_maker_ask and exact account side; preserve that role's venue/integrator integer fields. Only two explicitly zero components prove zero without a unit conversion. Missing/malformed components or nonzero integers with unverified venue units remain UNKNOWN. Never copy a generic fee schedule or account tier into an actual receipt. Already normalized evidenced quote-currency fees remain supported.
- For complete initial-flat to final-flat execution, per-account gross PnL is SELL notionals minus BUY notionals, including every fallback. Count each trade once per account/market; reciprocal own receipts count on both accounts. Require complete causal fill/position history and exact zero net quantity. Net execution PnL subtracts all proved receipt fees; missing fees permit GROSS_ONLY, never a net-profit claim. Known terminal no-fill legs have zero execution fees. Open/unresolved/incomplete cycles have no closed PnL.
- Funding-inclusive total is separate and UNKNOWN without attributable funding evidence. Account-reported realized PnL may include funding and is not substituted for execution PnL. Unknown amounts are not zero; tiny results retain decimal precision. Economics does not alter dispatch, matching, inventory or controller admission gates.
