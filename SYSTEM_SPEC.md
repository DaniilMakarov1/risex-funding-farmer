# ACK execution and storage contract — 2026-09-26 audit

This section supersedes the admission and persistence statements below; older sections are retained as historical assertions. Owner execution through Telegram and the simple launcher supports ACK. `/run`, `/run ack T [N]`, and the one/several-MARKET menu remain available; `/run ws` and `--receiver-admission ws_confirmed` cannot launch. A legacy saved `ws_confirmed` default is interpreted as ACK in the simple/control local-validation path without rewriting the configuration bytes bound to controller state. Fan-out is ACK only. Legacy one-to-one library admission branches and historical journal readers remain for compatibility; the WebSocket account/book transport remains necessary for ACK and is not a selectable execution mode.

An application ACK may omit an exchange order ID. A local source observation can supply that ID only when its account, client index, market, side, type, time in force, reduce-only flag, price and exact untouched quantity match the prepared source plan. An ID supplied by the ACK still has to match. Positive ACK and fresh local book veto do not prove source priority or matching with our accounts.

Write-ahead persistence. Every mutation intent, authorization/settings boundary, unknown event and terminal result remains durable before the dependent action. All prepared receiver intents in one handoff are appended in order and flushed together, once, before any MARKET send. This disk wait runs off the event loop. After it returns, the engine checks prepared deadlines, pre-LIMIT account freshness, the source-to-receiver bound and current local stream/book/source evidence again; it makes no extra network request. A refusal sends no MARKET, records `accepted: false, not_sent: true`, and follows source cancellation and reconciliation. An intent without complete terminal evidence remains unresolved after interruption and is never replayed. A disk failure poisons the writer; no member of an unflushed batch may send.

ACK attempt/cycle journals defer only the explicit `EXECUTION_DIAGNOSTICS` allowlist while holding the exclusive journal lock. The pending prefix is flushed at the next durable event, at 64 records or 256 KiB, or before releasing the lock. Unknown event names default to synchronous durability. Thus plan/observation/result diagnostics can share a barrier with the following mutation; completion is never merely queued. A newly created execution journal also syncs its parent directory. Stream diagnostic rows are queued and written in batches; session start and end are synced, with a missing end still meaning incomplete evidence. Cancellation drains an in-flight disk worker before closing its descriptor. These stream rows do not authorize orders.

Every exit from paired closing preparation releases unused nonce reservations before residual recovery, including exhausted price selection and early returns. Cancellation reuses an exact source observation just obtained by the post-MARKET poll, avoiding an immediately repeated lookup; an older pre-MARKET observation still requires a fresh read. Final per-account reconciliation remains sequential. Existing receipt identity, funding, unknown-outcome, no-replay and exact-zero requirements remain in force.

Timing now records the source-to-MARKET send boundary after the disk barrier, the ACK-to-MARKET send interval and the receiver intent durability wait. Missing send timing is unavailable, not zero. Cards prefer the actual send interval, falling back to the old intent interval only for old journals. An offline report resolves an intended-but-unsent MARKET only with a matching explicit pre-send refusal and complete zero-mutation receipts; it does not invent an exchange ACK for this case. Runtime implementation identity now covers fan-out, random-cycle and wallet selection code.

Terminal reason text follows the inventory classification. Zero final account snapshots accompanied by incomplete execution/history evidence are described as zero snapshots with incomplete final inventory proof; they must not say "final inventory confirmed flat" while the inventory field is UNKNOWN. This applies to one-to-one and fan-out cycles and does not relax the stop or reconciliation rules.

Correction of the historical dust statement below: the existing bound Robinhood BTC recovery already allows an exact, on-grid below-minimum position to be closed reduce-only. Fan-out uses that same rule; it does not add a top-up or permit oversized orders. Other bindings and unproved/invalid residuals still stop. This audit adds a regression for the actual BTC binding, without changing that policy.

# One LIMIT filled by several MARKETs (fan-out) (2026-09-26)

Enabling. A bare Telegram `/run` while idle first asks the mode with two one-time reply buttons, "1 LIMIT → 1 MARKET" and "1 LIMIT → несколько MARKET" (the answer is matched exactly after whitespace/case normalization, `->` equals `→`; any other plain text, including a number, keeps the question with a hint), then the cycle count with the unchanged ForceReply question; each question is valid 300 s from when it was asked, is kept in memory only and follows the existing cancellation, renewal, owner and no-replay rules. The count answer launches exactly `/run ack 1 N`, plus `fanout: true` in the saved launch options for the second button; every cycle of that series then runs the child `simple --keychain --no-progress --config … --receiver-admission ack --price-improvement-ticks 1 --fanout`. The acceptance names the mode. The explicit forms `/run ack|ws T [N]` and `/run N` stay 1 → 1. `simple --fanout` refuses before claiming a slot unless the admission is ACK or WS, and records `WALLET_POOL` in a claimed slot without `wallets.json`; `--fanout` is accepted by `simple` only. Without the flag every behavior is the unchanged 1 → 1 contract.

Draw (per cycle, public reads only). The pool eligibility is unchanged. A wallet is receiver-ready when its available balance covers the pool requirement formula for ⌈minimum × 1.10⌉ (one receiver part); its source capacity is the largest k (2 ≤ k ≤ ready − 1, at most 16) whose requirement for ⌈k × minimum × 1.10⌉ it covers. The source is uniform among receiver-ready wallets with capacity ≥ 2, k is uniform in [2, min(ready − 1, capacity, 16)] and the receivers are drawn uniformly without replacement in drawn order; the direction (the receivers' exposure) is uniform. With fewer than three receiver-ready wallets or no funded source the slot records `WALLETS_UNAVAILABLE` and nothing is read with a key or sent. `launch.json` keeps `random_route` (source, first receiver, direction), `fanout_receivers` (receivers 2..k) and `wallet_selection` with `mode: "fanout"`, `pair: null` and `fanout` (receiver-ready list, requirements, capacities, source, receivers); the cycle refuses a reservation whose `fanout_receivers` differ from its configuration. The configuration binds `extra_receiver_account_indices` (present only for a fan-out); the SDK client signs, reserves nonces, sets leverage and prepares cancellations only for the source and these receivers, and the read stream subscribes to every one of them (order roles `source`, `receiver`, `receiver_2` … `receiver_16`).

Sizing. One part's minimum is the larger of the base minimum and the quote minimum at the LIMIT price, in size ticks. Q is uniform on ticks in [⌈k × part minimum × 1.10⌉, min(source cap, Σ receiver caps)] (caps at the owner's 4x or stricter venue fraction with the fee, adverse-price and 0.10 planning-reserve terms); the split draws k − 1 uniform cut points over the ticks above the k minimums, redraws up to 64 times until every part fits its receiver's cap, otherwise fills the room in receiver order; every part is at least one minimum and Σ parts = Q. Draw order: Q, hold, cuts. Leverage is the minimal sufficient fraction per account (4x cap). The fresh pre-dispatch revalidation keeps every part at or above the fresh minimum and Q at or above the fresh ⌈k × minimum × 1.10⌉; a shortfall shrinks parts (largest first, never below a minimum) only while Q stays at that floor, first with the planning then with the dispatch reserve, otherwise the cycle stops before any order.

Attempt. One handoff: the source's POST_ONLY LIMIT of Q and k IOC MARKETs (worst price = the LIMIT price) of the parts, all prepared (signed, nonce reserved) before the LIMIT; the unchanged ACK or WS admission and L2 veto; every `RECEIVER[_N]_DISPATCH_INTENT` is journaled before any receiver send, then all MARKETs are sent concurrently; each is observed to a terminal state, the source remainder is cancelled and every leg is reconciled from its own receipts. A receiver is own-matched only through reciprocal trade, order and account identities with the source; the attempt is MATCHED only when every receiver filled exactly its part against the source and the source filled Q. A zero-mutation admission refusal is retried within the unchanged budget; a resumed fan-out attempt journal with an unresolved mutation is refused (operator recovery scans every `RECEIVER_N_DISPATCH_INTENT`).

Outcomes. An opening that is not fully matched gets no hold: every account's residual goes at once to the per-account reduce-only recovery (🟡 when it ends flat). Any unknown or unresolved leg (dispatch outcome unknown, a rejected MARKET whose order is not proved) makes the attempt UNKNOWN and, as for 1 → 1, no automatic write follows; known positions stay for `/close` and the series stops. Closing mirrors the opening with the source's reduce-only LIMIT of Σ closed parts and the concurrent reduce-only MARKETs of every receiver whose confirmed position is at least one minimum (at least two, else CLOSING_BLOCKED), with the unchanged 15-attempt budget; every remainder goes to the reduce-only recovery over all k + 1 accounts. A residual below the venue minimum is refused as today (KNOWN_RESIDUAL, not flat); fan-out parts sit near the minimum, so a partial fill leaves such dust more often than 1 → 1.

Evidence and views. Plans carry the legs `receiver_2` … and a `fanout` block (accounts, parts, positions, identities); receipts carry every receiver leg with its own match; `CYCLE_COMPLETE` carries `remaining_positions` per role. The offline report projects these legs, requires terminal proof and causal positions for every role (inventory keys `receiver_2` …), sums the receivers in each phase row (with a `receivers` list) and counts turnover and PnL over all accounts. Telegram: the wallet step names LIMIT and MARKET wallets (or why the fan-out could not start), the selection and accepted-LIMIT steps list every receiver with its part, execution notices count each receiver's own match, the card shows `LIMIT A SIDE → MARKET B part + C part SIDE` and every account's position on a stop, and series and 24-hour totals include every account of a fan-out cycle ("Оборот всех счетов" once a fan-out cycle is summed).

# Stops before the source send and unsent prepared orders (2026-09-26)

When a paired attempt ends, every order it prepared but never handed to the transport is invalidated, which releases its nonce reservation inside the SDK (a sent order was consumed before the transport and is unaffected). Without this, a stop before the source send left the prepared source order holding the account's nonce; with API nonce management the venue keeps returning that same next nonce, so a retry or reduce-only recovery on that account was refused (cycle-312). An attempt that stops before its source send for a pre-dispatch reason — pre-source stream refusal, order preparation failure before source exposure, source quote latency expiry — and whose legs reconcile with nothing dispatched, no order, no trade, zero fill, complete history, no leg unknown reason and unchanged positions is FAILED_PREFLIGHT_BLOCKED, never UNKNOWN. When every such reason other than the stream is transient (a preparation timeout or connection failure, which includes an expired pre-quote nonce reservation, or a quote-age expiry) the guard records `pre_dispatch` TRANSIENT (otherwise FAILED) and the attempt is retryable within the unchanged opening (6) / paired-closing (15) budgets after one poll-interval settle (`stream_settle_seconds`); a non-transient one, or budget exhaustion, ends the phase and the existing reduce-only recovery closes the proved inventory (cycle-317). A dead stream (UNAVAILABLE) is still not retried; anything that could have sent stays UNKNOWN. Display: a cycle card says the residual was closed reduce-only "не полностью" unless the inventory is CONFIRMED_FLAT, and a flat account line reads "позиции нет".

# Owner /stop (2026-09-26)

Telegram `/stop` passes the existing owner-command checks, is never replayed and is accepted at any time. It sets an in-memory stop and writes the owner-only marker `stop-request.json` next to the configuration (atomic; presence alone is the request). While a stop is pending the controller claims and launches no cycle; an inter-cycle pause or check-retry wait ends at once and a between-cycle check is not retried. The simple cycle launcher refuses to claim a slot while the marker exists. A running cycle observes the marker only at safe points: after SELECTION_PROVED before any leverage write and after preparation before the first order it journals `OWNER_STOP_OBSERVED` and ends FAILED_PREFLIGHT_BLOCKED with the fixed reason `owner /stop before the first order; no order was sent`; after a retryable zero-fill opening attempt it makes no further attempt (any fill goes to the unchanged reduce-only residual closure); during the hold it polls every second and, on a stop, journals `HOLD_ENDED_BY_OWNER_STOP` (selected and held seconds) and proceeds to the unchanged paired closing and residual recovery. Submissions, cancellations, reconciliation, closing and `close-positions` never observe the marker; without a stop hook the hold is unchanged. The offline report accepts a hold shorter than selected only with that row for the same hold between HOLD_ANCHORED and CLOSING_PLAN_READY; otherwise INCOMPLETE_HOLD_EVIDENCE remains.

After the running operation (admission, run, series or close) ends, the controller records a stopped series (a claimed but unlaunched step becomes NOT_LAUNCHED; a finished step keeps its result), then proves CLOSE_READY on every wallet with the bounded check retry; nonzero positions get one existing reduce-only close under a durable `active` close record, which must be CONFIRMED_FLAT; then a fresh READY must prove exact zero with no orders, which clears `active` and refreshes `last_recovery`. Any failure keeps the barrier, reports a fixed stage (STOP_CLOSE_READY/STOP_CLOSE/STOP_READY) and category and retries nothing else. The result (`update_id`, request/completion time, FLAT or NOT_PROVED, close slot/status, failure fields) is saved as `last_stop`, logged to stderr on failure, shown as a Telegram result and a `/status` line until the next `/run`. The marker is removed when the stop completes and synchronously before every fresh `/run` (a `/run` that cannot remove it is refused); a leftover can only stop a cycle before its first order. A duplicate `/stop` during a stop is answered without a second procedure. The bot keeps running; prices, sizing, leverage choice, admission, closing algorithms and other series rules are unchanged.

# Saved intent proofs and bounded between-cycle check retry (2026-09-26)

History resolution (controller CLOSE_READY/READY, the cycle child's own check, closure and the pilot) first consults `recovery-intents.jsonl` next to the configuration: an owner-only append-only shared journal of `INTENT_RESOLUTION_CHECKPOINT` rows (proof version 1: original journal path and SHA-256, intent time, exact plan, expected order ID, observed terminal order). A creation intent without terminal proof in its own journal is resolved by a saved row only when path and SHA-256 equal the current journal bytes and the saved plan, expected order ID and intent time equal the history; the saved order must still pass the live rules (identity/parameters, terminal status, LIMIT price) and have been observed not before the intent and not after the row. A row naming the current journal bytes that fails any rule blocks (PreflightBlocked); rows of another proof version are ignored; other journal bytes are proved live again. Only intents left without proof count toward the live-lookup bound (`max_poll_count`); each is proved exactly as before (exact read, then bounded inactive-order history, fresh, observed after the intent) and every fresh proof is appended, also when a later lookup of the same check fails (a failure to save it then never replaces the original error). A terminal order never becomes executable again, so a saved exact proof replaces a new deep history lookup; historical journals and `recovery-checks.jsonl` (still only CURRENT_STATE_VERIFIED/LEVERAGE_RESOLUTION_CHECKPOINT) are unchanged, and code without this rule never reads the new file. Proofs report `resolved_now` (live) and `resolved_by_checkpoint`.

Between cycles of one saved `/run` (the next series step's CLOSE_READY and READY, and the fresh READY after a pre-cycle close) the controller repeats a read-only check that failed only because a read did not complete: timeout, connection or payload failure, typed HTTP 429, or the engine's retryable read failure, identified on the exception or its explicit causes. Three attempts in total, 10 s and 20 s apart (a larger typed Retry-After up to 60 s), each the complete check, with one Telegram step per retry and one stderr line. Every other failure (any other PreflightBlocked refusal, invalid proof, configuration, credential, lock, file or HTTP error) and cancellation stop as before; nothing launches without a fresh complete READY, and a close is never repeated. First-step admission keeps its single check. A stopped run saves `last.stop` (stage SERIES_CLAIM/SERIES_PAUSE/SERIES_CLOSE_READY/SERIES_READY/PRE_CYCLE_CLOSE/POST_CLOSE_READY/LAUNCH/CLOSE/FINISH, fixed category, exception class name, attempts for checks, elapsed seconds in the stage, time), prints it as one credential-free stderr line, and names the stage and attempts in the Telegram stop line. Trading, sizing, prices, leverage, series count, pauses, no-replay and flatness rules are unchanged.

# Owner wallet pool and random pair per cycle (2026-09-26)

Optional `wallets.json` next to the operator configuration (`{"schema":"hood-wallet-pool-v1","wallets":[...],"paused":[...]}`; owner-only regular file, no symlink, at most 256 KiB, positive unique account indices, at most 1000 wallets, at least two active, the configured source and receiver always included). Without the file every behavior below is the unchanged two-account contract. The file holds no credentials; each wallet's key is the Keychain record bound to API origin, environment, chain, account and the configured key index. The file is re-read by every readiness check and every cycle; changing it needs no controller restart.

`./wallet add N` (terminal only) takes the key from hidden input or reuses a stored one, proves it in memory with one authenticated read of account N (identity, active status, zero selected-market position, no selected-market orders), stores a new key in Keychain only after that proof, and appends N atomically under its own lock. A position, orders, inactive status or unproved key refuse the wallet and store nothing. `pause`/`resume` change only selection; there is no removal (history on a wallet keeps it in the pool). `list` is offline.

Readiness with a pool: CLOSE_READY, READY and startup recovery first require a stored key for every pool wallet (active and paused), accept historical creation and leverage intents on any pool wallet (and still refuse any other account), prove each unresolved intent exactly as before, then read every wallet with bounded concurrency (4) using the same identity, authorization, readiness, no-active-order, margin-evidence and freshness rules as the pair reads. READY requires every wallet exactly flat. The proof keeps `source`/`receiver` (the configured pair) and adds one row per wallet (`accounts`) and the pool (`wallets`); the shared journal stores compact per-wallet rows.

Cycle: after local validation the child reads the market and the public state of every active wallet (no key value is read, nothing is signed) and marks a wallet ready when the read succeeds, the account is active, its selected-market position is exactly zero, a key is stored and its available balance covers one venue-minimum order at 4x (or a stricter venue minimum fraction), the higher published/observed fee rate at the ask, the worse ask/bid distance to mark, 5% margin and the 0.10 planning reserve. Only a typed HTTP 429 on these public reads is retried, with the engine's bounded cooldown (poll count, reconcile timeout, Retry-After); any other read failure skips that wallet. Two distinct ready wallets are drawn uniformly (every ordered pair is one of n(n-1) equally likely integer draws), then the unchanged four-way role/direction draw. The draw (ready list, skipped wallets with reasons LOW_BALANCE/NOT_FLAT/KEY_MISSING/UNAVAILABLE, balances, requirement, pair) is stored inside the slot's immutable `launch.json`. With fewer than two ready wallets the slot records `WALLETS_UNAVAILABLE` and no order, key read or leverage change happens; an invalid pool file records `WALLET_POOL`. The drawn pair then runs the unchanged cycle; only its keys are loaded, while exact history lookups may use any pool wallet's stored key.

Closure with a pool (`/close`, pre-cycle closure, `close-positions`): the baseline covers every wallet; wallets holding positions are paired (an odd one with a flat wallet) and each pair runs the existing reduce-only residual loop through a client bound to exactly that pair after a fresh pair read. The first pair with an unproved outcome stops further pairs. The result lists every wallet; only all-zero proves `CONFIRMED_FLAT`.

Telegram: `/accounts` lists every pool wallet compactly (attention first, bounded), readiness steps and `/status` name the wallet scope, a live step shows the draw and skips, launch-failure cards explain `WALLETS_UNAVAILABLE`/`WALLET_POOL`, admission refusals name `WALLET_KEY_MISSING`/`WALLET_POOL`, and a later pool closure covering a cycle's pair is matched to it.

# History bounds, opening resize and pre-source stream refusals (2026-09-25)

Historical scans stream every journal line (2 MiB line bound, 100000 lines). A per-slot trading journal is bounded at 32 MiB; the shared `recovery-checks.jsonl` and `recovery-intents.jsonl` at 512 MiB each; at most 10000 journals are scanned. Reaching a size/count bound raises HistoryBoundExceeded (a PreflightBlocked), reported as admission category and launch code HISTORY_LIMIT; symlinks remain PreflightBlocked unsafe. CURRENT_STATE_VERIFIED and CLOSE_BASELINE rows persist `inputs_count` and `inputs_sha256` (SHA-256 of the canonical JSON list of {path, sha256} sorted by path) instead of the full list; the in-memory proof is unchanged. Existing rows are not rewritten.

On any opening preparation (initial or after a proved zero-fill opening attempt) with both positions equal to the initial baseline, identity unchanged, confirmed leverage unchanged and the size grid unchanged, a fresh budget failure or owner-cap excess may reduce quantity: per leg, unit cost = fresh total required / quantity at the confirmed IMF, fresh mark/executable prices and conservative fee/adverse bounds; the new tick is the smaller of the planning-reserve (initial_quote) fit, raised to the venue minimum when necessary, and the dispatch-reserve fit, selected tick and fresh 4x owner cap. It is journaled as OPENING_QUANTITY_RECALCULATED, consumes one shared opening attempt and is fully revalidated before any order. It never grows, redraws, changes hold or leverage, applies to closing or to the fixed pilot. An unfundable venue minimum or missing evidence still stops. This supersedes the earlier fixed-quantity preparation rule.

Before the paired source send, a `begin_ws_admission` refusal records `pre_source_stream` TRANSIENT (typed TransientStreamContractError: stale local book or an own order still shown active) or UNAVAILABLE. With nothing sent on either account, the attempt is FAILED_PREFLIGHT_BLOCKED; ACK_ADMISSION_STOP is added only after an attempted source send. A TRANSIENT attempt whose legs reconcile with no order, trade or position change and a single pre-source reason is retryable within the unchanged opening (6) / paired-closing (15) budgets after one poll-interval settle (`stream_settle_seconds` on PAIR_ATTEMPT_RETRY); the next attempt performs complete fresh preparation. Otherwise the phase ends and existing reduce-only recovery handles proved inventory.

# Reconciliation HTTP 429 and series spacing (2026-09-25)

The bound child account adapter preserves authoritative typed HTTP 429 without recording an identity mismatch. Plain read-only HTTP transport exposes 429 without parsing or logging its body and never retries itself. No automatic reserve/setting increase, quantity redraw or price widening follows a refusal. The choice is not a proved optimum and does not guarantee venue admission or matching. Opening reserve never blocks explicit reduce-only residual closure. This policy requires a reviewed operator-file update and runtime activation separately from Git publication.
5. Select an exclusive one-tick source improvement strictly inside the valid two-sided spread; receiver bound equals source price. A one-tick spread that merely joins existing volume is nonqualifying. Recheck exact grids, minimum quantity/notional, available balance, confirmed fraction and source/receiver bound compatibility; never round exposure upward.
6. Place source first; admit receiver only through the exact checks below. HOLD begins only after full reciprocal matching of the selected quantity between the two own orders is proved. Expected positions reached through outside trades do not authorize HOLD.
7. Wait using monotonic elapsed time from persisted confirmation anchor. At the deadline attempt paired closure with reversed sides, both reduce-only, fresh mirrored source price and equal receiver bound. The hold deadline is planned closing start, not a promise of full closure.
8. Reconcile every mutation. Fully known incomplete opening skips HOLD and uses existing residual closure. Preserve strategy failure even if residual recovery ultimately proves both accounts flat. Stop after this cycle; no automatic next cycle or replay after restart.

## Preparation, admission and timing

Before each final quote, overlap independent metadata/account reads and reserve one unsent nonce per configured account/key. SDK account state and active orders overlap; keep the account response's original timestamp. Fetch final book after validated context, then recheck its ages. The child consumes this exact preflight context once without duplicate post-quote account requests. Sign both exact final plans before exposing source when preparation is supported.

Prepared mutations stay in memory, owned by the original adapter/account/key and immutable plan; reserve/consume once. Unsent reservations expire within freshness bounds and release on failure/cancellation. Drain sibling tasks before cleanup. Possibly transmitted nonces remain blocked. Serialize signing/nonce/cancellation within each account/key domain. No prepared payload, token or secret enters durable evidence.

Receiver admission needs fresh independently read accounts and a two-sided public book, then a final exact source lookup. Source must be active, zero-fill and match account, market, order/client IDs, side, LIMIT/POST_ONLY, reduce-only flag, price and full remaining quantity. Its public level must identify that exact order/owner/side/price/quantity. Better external volume is LOST; same-price volume without explicit FIFO proof, anonymous/missing/stale/future/malformed/conflicting evidence is UNKNOWN. Both block receiver. An already-filled source always blocks receiver; no opening chase.

The HCR-42 timing view reuses local phase durations and separately reports each proof's age at guard/receiver send. Visibility-window duration overlaps private order, account and public-book reads; it is not the publication instant of a public level. A final private lookup does not renew public price-priority proof. No stream replacement or faster live outcome is established by the candidate.

One bounded refresh may resolve specifically pre-visibility missing source evidence only when earlier observations prove correct identities, unchanged positions, readiness, margin, freshness and no conflicting orders/fills. If the source account already identifies the exact resting source and only the public level is absent, refresh only the book; retain both account snapshots and their original ages. Otherwise refresh accounts and book together. Contradictory evidence remains sticky. Final admission revalidates all observations and reads the exact source again. Final public-book age participates in a monotonic send deadline with account, metadata, exact-order and prepared evidence, including nonce/signing/transmission time. Expiry does not authorize replay.

Explicit valid overrides remain supported. Optional max_quote_age_seconds and max_source_to_receiver_seconds are absent by default and, if configured, positive and no greater than freshness. Offline timing does not calibrate live thresholds.

Preparation and safe zero-fill paired retries share at most 6 total attempts for opening and 15 for paired closing, including the first attempt. The confirmed pilot stays at one attempt per phase; separate residual recovery is unchanged. Retry transient read/preparation failures only before possible mutation; deterministic configuration/identity/auth/state/grid/minimum failures stop. After source exposure, only complete terminal zero-fill/empty-history reconciliation, unchanged original positions, no receiver dispatch, no ambiguous mutation and no other barrier permit a fresh pair attempt after exact source cancellation or canceled-post-only. Use fresh quote, new IDs and separate child journal. Arbitrary cancellations and partial fills do not qualify. Closing retries retain the nonzero paired baseline.

## Reconciliation and residual closure

Persist and fully synchronize mutation intent before a single send without redirects/implicit retries. Accepted response/tx hash does not prove execution. Exception/timeout after send entry, malformed/conflicting response or missing terminal is unresolved; no dependent write on either account. Exact known application rejection and local pre-send failure are distinct. Restart reads/reconciles; it never replays mutations or advances automatically.

Only cancel the exact positively identified own source. Count cancellation-race fills. Require bounded complete order/trade/account history, exact deduplication, terminal order and causal position agreement. Temporarily propagating observations may be reread within existing limits; identity/contract failures remain barriers. An absent source or a source filled during cancellation after a failed public-book guard can become known source-only PARTIAL only after full terminal order/trade/position reconciliation and an unchanged undispatched receiver. Preserve the admission reason, but do not classify proved execution as UNKNOWN merely because that reason contains UNKNOWN. Unrelated identity, history, cancellation or transport uncertainty still blocks dependent writes.

Retain a positively observed source order ID when a later exact lookup temporarily returns no order, including when the accepted response has no order ID. Reconcile history against that retained ID; a conflicting later ID remains a barrier. Missing visibility never permits receiver dispatch or proves cancellation.

After complete reconciliation, close freshly proved residuals with reduce-only bounded MARKET/IOC, fresh executable-side prices and unique IDs. Cancel/reconcile a remaining own limit first. Reconcile each attempt before sizing the next actual residual; never overlap unresolved orders. Pace terminal zero-fill attempts by the existing poll interval and serve both accounts fairly. Fully reconciled partial fills have no fixed total attempt cap. Exchange rejection, ineligible below-minimum residual, invalid/stale/ambiguous state, failed request or interruption stops dependent writes and preserves PARTIAL/UNKNOWN. No widening or size increase/reversal.

HCR-41 reserves a nonce before the residual-close book read when supported, rechecks the exact account, and binds signing and transport to the earliest evidence deadline. A known local nonce/signing failure blocks only that account after fresh two-account identity/position reconciliation; the independent account may still close. An uncertain send is never replayed. Transport and terminal/reconciliation phase timings are separate diagnostics, not venue latency or a live fill guarantee. Leverage intents record signed hash/nonce/account/key/type before send, preserving the pinned SDK's exact 80-character bare hexadecimal hash without adding a prefix or normalizing case. A proven pre-transport failure, including cancellation during nonce/signing/lock preparation, is journaled as NOT_SENT; cancellation still propagates. Cancellation after possible sendTx entry stays UNKNOWN. An uncertain setting can clear only after bounded read-only proof of exact terminal executed or failed transaction identity, consumed API-key nonce and fresh flat/no-active-order account state; executed status also requires the exact requested setting readback. Missing mark or a changed budget blocks before send. Published fees and this model do not prove the venue's complete admission rule; no discretionary reserve is enabled.

Reduce-only paired and residual closing requires fresh authorized account/position/order identity, but does not compare existing `cross_initial_margin_requirement` with remaining `available_balance` or require new opening margin. A close is still subject to the venue's own acceptance, price bound and full causal reconciliation.

Journal the exception, quantity and current minimums. All opening/paired checks and other markets retain their minimum gates. Never round a residual up; rejection/unknown execution still stops that operation normally.

Recovery account reads may repeat only classified transport/time-out failures within the existing reconciliation timeout, poll interval and maximum poll count. Drain parallel reads, retain identity failures even alongside transport errors, and never repeat a mutation. Revalidate quote freshness after a delayed pre-send account read. Preserve the specific local refusal in terminal evidence, including failures before the first closing intent.

## Result and accounting contract

- Paired SUCCESS requires full exact quantity on both legs and reciprocal receipts agreeing on trade ID, accounts, order IDs, price, quantity and opposite sides; supplied client identities must agree. Missing/unproved/external quantity cannot become own matching. Full normal-cycle SUCCESS additionally requires successful opening and closing and terminal inventory proof. CLOSE_REOPEN retains its separate exposure semantics.
- CONFIRMED_FLAT requires resolved terminal order histories and fresh causal exact-zero positions on both accounts; snapshots or process exit alone are insufficient. Known residual is PARTIAL, unresolved execution UNKNOWN. Fees and matching do not replace inventory evidence.
- Receipts retain actual quantities/prices and fee evidence. At the official SDK boundary, derive maker/taker role from strict is_maker_ask and exact account side; preserve that role's venue/integrator integer fields. Only two explicitly zero components prove zero without a unit conversion. Missing/malformed components or nonzero integers with unverified venue units remain UNKNOWN. Never copy a generic fee schedule or account tier into an actual receipt. Already normalized evidenced quote-currency fees remain supported.
- For complete initial-flat to final-flat execution, per-account gross PnL is SELL notionals minus BUY notionals, including every fallback. Count each trade once per account/market; reciprocal own receipts count on both accounts. Require complete causal fill/position history and exact zero net quantity. Net execution PnL subtracts all proved receipt fees; missing fees permit GROSS_ONLY, never a net-profit claim. Known terminal no-fill legs have zero execution fees. Open/unresolved/incomplete cycles have no closed PnL.
- Funding-inclusive total is separate and UNKNOWN without attributable funding evidence. Account-reported realized PnL may include funding and is not substituted for execution PnL. Unknown amounts are not zero; tiny results retain decimal precision. Economics does not alter dispatch, matching, inventory or controller admission gates.
