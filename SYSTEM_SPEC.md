## Result and accounting contract

- Paired SUCCESS requires full exact quantity on both legs and reciprocal receipts agreeing on trade ID, accounts, order IDs, price, quantity and opposite sides; supplied client identities must agree. Missing/unproved/external quantity cannot become own matching. Full normal-cycle SUCCESS additionally requires successful opening and closing and terminal inventory proof. CLOSE_REOPEN retains its separate exposure semantics.
- CONFIRMED_FLAT requires resolved terminal order histories and fresh causal exact-zero positions on both accounts; snapshots or process exit alone are insufficient. Known residual is PARTIAL, unresolved execution UNKNOWN. Fees and matching do not replace inventory evidence.
- Receipts retain actual quantities/prices and fee evidence. At the official SDK boundary, derive maker/taker role from strict is_maker_ask and exact account side; preserve that role's venue/integrator integer fields. Only two explicitly zero components prove zero without a unit conversion. Missing/malformed components or nonzero integers with unverified venue units remain UNKNOWN. Never copy a generic fee schedule or account tier into an actual receipt. Already normalized evidenced quote-currency fees remain supported.
- For complete initial-flat to final-flat execution, per-account gross PnL is SELL notionals minus BUY notionals, including every fallback. Count each trade once per account/market; reciprocal own receipts count on both accounts. Require complete causal fill/position history and exact zero net quantity. Net execution PnL subtracts all proved receipt fees; missing fees permit GROSS_ONLY, never a net-profit claim. Known terminal no-fill legs have zero execution fees. Open/unresolved/incomplete cycles have no closed PnL.
- Funding-inclusive total is separate and UNKNOWN without attributable funding evidence. Account-reported realized PnL may include funding and is not substituted for execution PnL. Unknown amounts are not zero; tiny results retain decimal precision. Economics does not alter dispatch, matching, inventory or controller admission gates.
