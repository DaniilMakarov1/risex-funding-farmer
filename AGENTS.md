## Context and sequential handoff

Rotate proactively at substantive checkpoints or first visible context warning, or when accumulated logs/corrections obscure decisions. Use CONTEXT_HANDOFF_REQUIRED; do not invent percentages or poll for them. Repeated compaction is not the continuation plan and prompts cannot disable client compaction.

A full suite is not required solely for WIP rotation. Chief verifies preservation, stops predecessor, then activates successor on a fresh accepted-base branch/worktree with a reproducible transfer of the unaccepted patch. Never merge WIP for rotation.

Chief normally hands off clean published main after acceptance. Earlier handoff is allowed with a separately saved WIP_NOT_ACCEPTED/READY_FOR_REVIEW candidate only when Builder/process writes have stopped and ownership is explicit; main must contain no unaccepted code. Predecessor records state and ceases control before successor becomes sole Chief. Preparing a new chat is not authority to activate it. No history fork or two concurrent Chiefs. If tools cannot safely stop/create/transfer, give ready handoff and exact manual actions; never simulate them.
